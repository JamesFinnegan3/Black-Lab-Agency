Case Study - Drop by Sol

The Problem

Drop by Sol is a hemp-based weight loss gummy brand operating in a highly regulated advertising category.

Before working with Black Lab, they faced ongoing challenges:

  • Repeated ad account shutdowns and rejections
  • Loss of professional accounts tied to advertising bans
  • Inconsistent campaign approvals and unreliable performance
  • Inability to collect meaningful conversion data due to ads not staying live

Without stable approvals, they couldn't scale traffic, optimize campaigns, or sustain growth.

The Solution

Drop by Sol partnered with Black Lab Agency to implement a compliance-first advertising system designed specifically for advertising restricted products.

Here's how we did it:

  • Built and deployed compliant advertising and routing system
  • Structured their funnel and landing experience to align with platform requirements
  • Guided content and messaging to reduce policy risk
  • Managed paid advertising with a focus on approval stability and performance

Within 48 hours, Drop by Sol was approved and actively running ads.
