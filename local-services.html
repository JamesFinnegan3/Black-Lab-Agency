Case Study - Finnegan Hauling

The Problem

Finnegan Hauling is a local hauling company providing bulk removal and moving services within a ~20-mile radius.

They had strong operators and great customer satisfaction, but no reliable way to generate consistent new business.

Growth had stalled because:

  • New jobs came from referrals and paper advertising
  • There was no structured online systems or advertising
  • The owner didn't have the time or expertise to manage paid ads or lead tracking
  • They needed predictable quote volume without taking on upfront risk

The Solution

We partnered with Finnegan Hauling, building them a custom advertising system under our performance-based model.

  • Built their full advertising and lead collection system at no upfront cost
  • Created and launched local ad campaigns to their target market in their service area
  • Implemented a clean quote-booking flow to collect leads immediately and send to the operators at Finnegan Hauling
  • Handled ongoing optimization to improve volume and quality of leads

Finnegan Hauling didn't pay us until the system brought real results.

18x
Return on Ad Spend

Because we focus on bringing the right leads, not just more leads, their team spent less time chasing bad inquiries and more time closing real jobs.

Case Study - Three Points Hospitality

The Problem

Three Points Hospitality is a Gainesville-based hospitality group operating two restaurant and bar locations: Oak and Cantina Añejo.

Despite strong locations and offerings, they faced several challenges:

  • Limited local awareness beyond existing regulars
  • Inconsistent traffic during seasonal slowdowns
  • Lack of high-quality, on-brand content showcasing their spaces, food, drinks and events
  • No scalable advertising system to drive foot traffic and awareness

They needed a way to stay top of mind locally and stabilize revenue throughout the year, especially during off-peak months.

The Solution

Three Points Hospitality partnered with Black Lab Agency to handle content production and full-scale local advertising.

We built and executed a complete advertising system that included:

  • High-impact photo and video content tailored for local discovery
  • Local awareness and traffic campaigns targeting local area
  • Paid media strategies focused on driving website visits and reservation bookings
  • Ongoing optimization to maximize return, not just impressions
  • Promotional and advertising consulting

Rather than relying on sporadic promotions, we created a system designed to generate consistent demand.

The Results

16x
Return on Ad Spend
18%
Year over year sales increase
25%
Sales increase during down season

By pairing strong creative with efficient local ad buying and discovery-based targeting, Oak and Cantina Añejo were able to drive growth when it mattered most.

Local restaurants and service locations don't just need ads — they need visibility, relevance, and consistency. By combining high-quality content with performance-focused local advertising, we can turn awareness into real, measurable sales growth, and make sure you never have a "slow season" again.
